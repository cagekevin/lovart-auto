## 一、提示词六要素（Establishing the Vision）

| 要素 | 原文说明 | 官方示例 |
|---|---|---|
| Subject 主体 | Who or what is in the image? Be specific. | a stoic robot barista with glowing blue optics |
| Composition 构图 | How is the shot framed? | extreme close-up, wide shot, low angle shot, portrait |
| Action 动作 | What is happening? | brewing a cup of coffee, mid-stride running through a field |
| Location 场景 | Where does the scene take place? | a futuristic cafe on Mars, a sun-drenched meadow at golden hour |
| Style 风格 | What is the overall aesthetic? | 3D animation, film noir, photorealistic, **1990s product photography** |
| Editing Instructions 编辑指令 | For modifying an existing image, be direct and specific. | change the man's tie to green, remove the car in the background |

## 二、细化细节（Refining the Details）

- **构图与画幅**："A 9:16 vertical poster," "A cinematic 21:9 wide shot."
- **相机与布光**："A low-angle shot with a shallow depth of field (f/1.8)," "Golden hour backlighting creating long shadows," "Cinematic color grading with muted teal tones."
- **文本植入**："The headline 'URBAN EXPLORER' rendered in bold, white, sans-serif font at the top."
- **事实约束**（图表类）："A scientifically accurate cross-section diagram."
- **参考图输入**："Use Image A for the character's pose, Image B for the art style, and Image C for the background environment."

## 三、与产品/电商直接相关的官方表述

1. 风格关键词可直接用 **1990s product photography**。
2. 产品 mockup："Sharp, legible text helps you create impactful posters, intricate diagrams, and even detailed product mockups."
3. 3D 包装/服装贴图（Tip 7）："Seamlessly drape patterns, logos, and artwork onto 3D objects and surfaces—from apparel to packaging—while preserving natural lighting and texture."
4. 电商服装示例："Combine these images into one appropriately arranged cinematic image in 16:9 format and change the dress on the mannequin to the dress in the image."

## 四、参考图规则（官方）

- 最多可输入 **14 张**图片合成；可混合 6–14 张无关图片并保持多物体一致性。
- 上传参考图必须清晰定义每张图的角色（姿势/风格/背景分工）。

## 五、官方明确的当前限制

- 小字号、精细细节、准确拼写可能不完美。
- 数据类图表、信息图需人工核查。
- 多语言生成可能出现语法错误。
- 高级编辑（混合、改光照）可能产生不自然瑕疵。
- 跨编辑的角色一致性可能波动。
